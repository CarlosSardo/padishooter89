HOW TO PLAY

It's a penalty shoot-out! 5 penalties each. Players swap between shooter and keeper every turn.

  • KEEPER: tap a zone in the goal to dive there (the shooter should look away!).
  • SHOOTER: tap a target zone, then tap again to lock the POWER bar and shoot.

Power 1-100%: more power is harder to save, but aim for the corners with full power and you might miss!

In 1 player mode you play the CPU. Most goals after 5 rounds wins. A tie goes to sudden death!

Listen for the referee whistle, the crowd, and the GOAL music. Tap the ♪ button (top-right) to mute.
